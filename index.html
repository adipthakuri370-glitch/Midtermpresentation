What is Dashain?

15 Days of Harmony

The longest and most anticipated national holiday in Nepal, spanning across two vibrant autumn weeks in September–October.

Divine Blessings

Celebrates Goddess Durga’s triumph over the demon Mahishasura, symbolizing light conquering moral darkness.

Grand Homecoming

Millions travel across the world and country to reunite with their elders, receive blessings, and enjoy traditional feasts.

Key Days of Dashain

Explore the major rituals observed throughout the festival

Day 01

Ghatasthapana

Planting Jamara

Sowing barley seeds in a holy clay pot filled with sacred river sand. Over 10 days, these sprout into golden-yellow shoots called Jamara.

Day 07

Phulpati

Sacred Procession

The "Day of Sacred Flowers" featuring an official military and cultural parade in Kathmandu displaying royal flora offerings.

Day 08 & 09

Maha Ashtami & Navami

Ayudha Puja

Worship of tools, vehicles, weapons, and machinery as expressions of gratitude for smooth daily operation and protection.

Day 10

Vijaya Dashami (Main Day)

Tika & Blessing

Elders apply Tika (red rice mix) and place yellow Jamara behind younger relatives' ears while giving heartfelt blessings and Dakshina.

Day 15

Kojagrat Purnima

Full Moon Conclusion

Honoring Goddess Laxmi on the full moon night, marking the official close of Dashain for the year.

Cultural Highlights

Linge Ping (Bamboo Swings)

Erected in almost every village. Popular folklore says everyone must leave the ground on a swing at least once during Dashain to wash away bad fortune.

Flying Kites

Sky filled with colorful kites serves as a symbolic signal asking Indra (God of Rain) to stop the monsoons for harvest season.

Festive Gastronomy

Savoring ring-shaped crispy rice bread (Sel Roti), rich goat curry, spiced pickles, and traditional home-made sweets with family.
